The code of Tentacle TV is free software under the GNU AGPL v3.0 or later
(see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS`](LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS)).
The **name and the logos are not**: they identify the official project and
its official builds, so that users always know what they are installing and
whom they are trusting with their media server.

This policy explains what you may do with them without asking.

## The marks

- the name **"Tentacle TV"**, and **"Tentacle"** when used as the name of a
  media application, server or client;
- the **octopus mascot** and every logo, app icon, banner and illustration
  generated from the [`brand/`](brand/) directory (including
  `tentacle-logo-pirate.svg` and the app icons of every platform);
- the purple-to-pink "Tentacle" wordmark.

All are owned by Damien Rouge ("Knaox").

## Always allowed — no need to ask

- **Referring to the project** truthfully: articles, reviews, tutorials,
  videos, conference talks, comparison tables, social media posts, wikis.
- **Saying that something works with Tentacle TV**: a plugin, a theme, a
  script or a hardware setup may say "for Tentacle TV", "works with Tentacle
  TV" or "compatible with Tentacle TV", provided it is not presented as an
  official part of the project.
- **Unmodified official builds**: redistributing, mirroring or packaging the
  official, unmodified releases (including Docker images re-tagged in your own
  registry) under their name and logo.
- **Packaging for a distribution or a NAS app catalog** (Debian, Arch, Nix,
  Unraid, TrueNAS, Synology…) with changes limited to what the packaging
  requires (paths, defaults, dependencies, security patches), as long as the
  package description says it is packaged by you and links to the project.
- **Self-hosting**: running Tentacle TV, modified or not, for yourself, your
  family or your friends, without offering it to the public under the
  Tentacle TV name.
- **Showing the logo next to a link** to the official project, website or
  repository, without altering it.

## Not allowed without written permission

- **Forks and modified versions offered to others** — distributed, published
  in an application store, or run as a public or commercial service — under
  the name "Tentacle TV" or "Tentacle", or with its logos or icons. Such a
  version must use a **different name and different logos**. It may (and,
  under the AGPL, must) say truthfully that it is "based on Tentacle TV".
- Publishing an application in **any application store** (App Store, Mac App
  Store, Google Play, Microsoft Store, LG Content Store…) under the Tentacle TV
  name or icon, unless it is the official build published by the project.
- Using the marks in a way that suggests **endorsement, partnership or
  official status** that does not exist, or that could confuse users about
  who publishes a build or operates a service.
- Using the marks in a **domain name**, a company name, a product name or an
  online account name (other than for the uses allowed above, such as a fan
  account clearly marked as unofficial).
- **Altering the logos** (colours, shape, mascot) or combining them with other
  marks, except for size and format conversions.
- Using the marks on **merchandise** for sale.

## Other projects' marks

Tentacle TV is an independent client and companion server for
[Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
Jellyfin project; "Jellyfin" is used only to state compatibility. The same
applies to every other name of a product or service mentioned in the
project (Apple, Google, Microsoft, LG, TMDB…), which belong to their
respective owners.

## Asking

If in doubt, or for anything not covered here, open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Knaox/Tentacle-TV/issues) or write to the
maintainer. Permission is usually granted for community, non-commercial uses
that cannot mislead users.

This policy may be updated; the version in the default branch of the
repository is the one in force.
